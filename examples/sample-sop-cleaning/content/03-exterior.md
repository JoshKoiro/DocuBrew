## Painted body and plastics

Clean painted metal and plastic panels with a diluted all-purpose cleaner on a soft cloth, then wipe with a cloth dampened in clean water. Avoid soaking decals and badges.

::: note
Test any cleaner on a hidden area first — some original finishes and decals lift easily.
:::
