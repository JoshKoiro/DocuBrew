## Purpose

This procedure describes a routine solvent cleaning of a manual typewriter's mechanism, followed by exterior care. It applies to {{ appliesTo }}, and was written against a {{ machine.example }}.

## Out of scope

- Escapement adjustment and mainspring tension (see a separate SOP).
- Platen or feed-roller replacement.
- Repainting or decal work.

::: warning Ventilation
Use solvents only in a well-ventilated space, away from open flame. Keep a lid on the solvent container between steps.
:::

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Type basket | The semicircle of type bars in front of the platen. |
| Segment | The slotted arc that guides each type bar to the printing point. |
| Escapement | The mechanism that advances the carriage one space per keystroke. |
