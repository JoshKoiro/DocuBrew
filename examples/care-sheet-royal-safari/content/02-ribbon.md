---
title: Changing the ribbon
---

::: steps
1. Note how the old ribbon runs through the guides and the vibrator (the small fork in front of the platen).
2. Lift out both spools and unthread the ribbon.
3. Fit the new spools the same way, with the ribbon coming off the outside of each spool.
4. Thread the ribbon through the guides and the vibrator, then take up any slack.
5. Type a test line to check that the ribbon advances and reverses.
:::

## If something goes wrong

If a key sticks, the carriage won't move, or letters print unevenly, don't force it. Get in touch and bring it back in.
