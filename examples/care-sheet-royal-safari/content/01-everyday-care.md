> This is a sample Brew that ships with DocuBrew. Change the machine details in `brew.yaml` to make a care sheet for any typewriter.

- **Keep it covered.** Close the case or use a dust cover when you're not typing. Dust is the main cause of sluggish keys.
- **Keep it dry.** Store it indoors, away from damp basements, garages and direct sun.
- **Don't oil it.** It was cleaned and lubricated where it needs it. Oil elsewhere collects dust and makes keys stick.
- **Lock the carriage** before carrying the machine.

## Cleaning the type

When letters like *e* and *o* start to fill in, brush the type slugs with a stiff, dry brush, working away from the platen. Wipe the outside with a soft, slightly damp cloth.

::: caution
Never spray cleaners or lubricants into the machine.
:::
