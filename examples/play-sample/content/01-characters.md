::: cast Characters
- **Elias** — a typewriter repairman, sixties. Patient. Talks to machines.
- **Mara** — a woman in her thirties, carrying her father's typewriter.
- **The Bell** — the bell over the shop door. Never speaks; often interrupts.
:::

*Time:* The present. Late autumn.

*Place:* A small repair shop on Mill Street. Shelves of typewriters in cases. A workbench under a single lamp.
